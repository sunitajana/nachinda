About School

Experience Our Campus: Inspiring Minds and Shaping Futures

Nachinda J.K. High School has to its credit seven decades and more of an enriching journey during which progress and achievements have reached incredible summits on the strength of an exceptional vision and mission, as well as an untiring effort to incorporate the new, without bypassing the old. In all these years, despite all the sweeping changes, the school remains rooted in its commitment to give the best education, infuse values into the students and build a better future.

About The Head Master

Ashok Kumar Maity (A.H.M. & Officiating H.M.)

Over the years, the school which welcomed twenty children on a distant summer day has spread its rainbow wings in much the same way as its students have. The journey has been a magical one, marked by splendid milestones. The comely mud built houses soon grew into newer, bigger buildings that could bring more and more children into its fold. The early faculty handed the batons over to a burgeoning number of teachers who were equally committed and fully trained to keep up with the changing times.

Why Nachinda J.K. High School, West Bengal

At Nachinda J.K. High School, Purba Medinipur, West Bengal, our motto is to craft an advanced institution of professional learning of national standard where pursuit of knowledge and excellence shall be of paramount importance, transcending the barriers of nationality, language, cultural plurality and religion. Holistic development of students to enable them to achieve success not just in professional field, but all-round success in all aspects of life, is the keystone of Nachinda J.K. High School, West Bengal.

Hostel

A hostel in a school is a residential facility given by the school for students who must stay on campus. Hostels are typically seen in boarding schools, universities, or other institutions where students travel from far away.

Annual Sports

Annual Sports Day is a highly anticipated occasion that honors the spirit of sportsmanship, promotes friendship, and displays student's athletic prowess.

Annual program

An annual program in a school is a large event that highlights students' abilities and achievements while bringing the school community together. It is frequently used as a stage for students to perform, celebrate, and reflect on the year's achievements..

Garden

A school garden is an excellent addition to the campus, providing kids with hands-on learning opportunities, raising environmental consciousness, and improving the school's aesthetics.
